Vialrack — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · JD Ventures LLC

Short version

Vialrack collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays in a local database on your own device, and the app does not send it anywhere.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your entries (vials, compounds, doses, schedules, lab values, check-ins) are written to a local database file on your device — on Windows under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vialrack, on Android inside the app's private storage.
  • There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing identifies you to us.
  • There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDK that transmits data.
  • Exports and backups are files you create yourself, saved wherever you choose.

Network access

Android: the app requests the INTERNET permission because it runs its own server on the device's loopback address (127.0.0.1) and displays it to you; Android requires that permission even for loopback sockets. The app's network security configuration refuses every non-loopback destination, so it cannot talk to any server.

Windows: the app binds 127.0.0.1 only. It makes no outbound requests. Calendar reminders are exported as an .ics file that your own calendar app reads.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share data, because we never receive any. Purchases are processed by the store or payment platform you buy through (for example Google Play, Microsoft Store, or Gumroad); their privacy policies govern the payment details you give them, not us. A licence key is delivered as text and contains no personal information.

Children

Vialrack is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone regardless of age.

Your control

Because all data is local, deleting the app's data directory (or uninstalling the app) removes everything permanently. Use the in-app export first if you want a copy.

Health information

Vialrack is a record-keeping tool, not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and the figures it calculates are arithmetic from values you enter. Consult a licensed clinician about any medical decision.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it; the current version always lives at this address.
