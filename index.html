Vialrack

A daily protocol tracker — vials, reconstitution math, syringe units, schedules, labs and check-ins. Windows and Android.

What it is

  • Reconstitution math and U-100 syringe-unit dosing, done for you.
  • Vial inventory, dilution tracking, and per-peptide schedules on one screen.
  • Day-by-day history that never resets, reminders as a calendar file, lab imports and check-ins.
  • Runs entirely on your device. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

Price

$19.99, once. Not a subscription. A 14-day trial runs first; after it, every record you have stays readable and exportable even without a key — only new entries pause.

Privacy

Vialrack collects nothing. Everything you enter is stored in a local database on your own device, and the app makes no network calls to anyone. See the privacy policy.

Not a medical device

Vialrack is record-keeping and arithmetic only. It does not diagnose, treat, or make clinical recommendations, and it is not a substitute for advice from a licensed clinician.
